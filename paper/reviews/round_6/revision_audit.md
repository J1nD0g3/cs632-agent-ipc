# Round-6 revision audit (meaning errors)

Checked 125 changed or new sentences of `paper/main.tex` against `cs632-store/main_pre_round6.tex`. That count includes the title, the captions, and every sentence with a changed macro. Each macro was traced through `paper_numbers.py` (`main_run`, `repeat`, `round4`, `round6`) to `results/` and `results_raw/`. vLLM KV sizes and flags were read from the round-6 server logs (`cs632-store/incoming/round6_raw.tar.gz`). All new sizes are 100,000 tokens, except 400,000 for `p11_c32_*`. Chunked prefill is on, with max_num_batched_tokens=8192.

These checks passed and are not listed below:
- `RSixWriteSigWord` and `RSixReadSigWord` (three each).
- `RSixWordingNullBound` (3.1 from 8B).
- The `RSixTime*` mean, min, and max values.
- `RSixHi*` and `ZcPooledDiff*` (N=300).
- `AAWorst` (2.2).
- The k=8 fan-out effects and DiD.
- The guard and heading recoveries.
- The 32B writing-control gap.
- The Fig. 3 caption against `fig_split.py` and `fig3_split.json`.
- No remaining reference to D, stage attribution, read_files, the Fact-checker, control labels, or `fig:loss`. Figs. 1, 2, and 4 are D-free.

Only items that need a fix are listed.
