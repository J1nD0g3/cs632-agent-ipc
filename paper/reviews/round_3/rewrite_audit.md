# Round 3 rewrite: meaning audit

Scope: abstract through conclusion plus the four captions, old (`cs632-store/main_pre_style.tex`) against new (`paper/main.tex`). After splitting on sentence ends, the old text has 122 sentences and the new text 192. 145 new sentences do not appear verbatim in the old text, and all 145 were checked against the old wording, `numbers.tex`, `paper_numbers.py`, `pipeline.py`, and the result files where needed. The captions are unchanged.

I checked these items and they need no fix:
- The Fact-checker in the pipe gets only the draft (`build_messages`, A2 branch: `prev = [before[-1]]`).
- C's Researchers are told to head each file with its questions (`INDEX_RULE["researcher"]`).
- Both 4B and 8B use 6 runs per server (one semaphore per server in `run.py`, `concurrency: 6` in both configs), so "no more cache pressure" holds.
- The Press ratios are computed tokens over tokens sent (`pressure()`).
- `PressAccMax` covers B and C, and zero-copy's change is 0.
- `WallFile*` covers B, C and D. `Forced*` covers the three named roles, and `Strict*` covers B, C and D.
- All `*SigWord` macros match their counts.
- `DelivB*` is measured against the facts in the notes (`alignfacts.py`).
- `FileSent*` covers B and C, matching "B and C send ...".
