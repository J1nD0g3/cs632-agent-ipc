Scope: `paper/main.tex` against `cs632-store/main_pre_round12.tex`. I read all 196 body and caption sentences
and checked the 126 that are changed or new. I also checked every Table 1 cell (5 effect rows and the rescue row
at 4 sizes) and the Figure 3 legend and panel labels. Macro values were checked against `numbers.tex`,

- The second family's Holm survivors (interaction at 4B, 14B, 32B, difference at 4B, 14B).
- The 8-22 lead at three sizes.
- The rescued vs kept accuracies and their counts.
- The budget (34/34) and cap (22/300 vs 1/300) numbers.
- The six-at-a-time median ratio of 0.99 (three runs).
- Both 33.9 values: t08_large gives 72.4 minus 38.5, and p07_k16_kv100k gives 71.9 minus 38.0. Both differences are 0.3385.
- The figure legend matches the arms.
- There are no semicolons in the text, and the paper does not mention a modified vLLM.
