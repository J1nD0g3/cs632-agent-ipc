# p18 meaning audit

Scope: diff of `cs632-store/main_pre_p18.tex` against `paper/main.tex`. Every `RFirst*` macro was recomputed from
`results/p18_first_14b/20261121-191515-8d096a4` with `round13_first` (without writing `numbers.tex`), and all values
match `numbers.tex` and the PDF. Directions are correct (`paired(a, b)` returns a - b; `RFirstRevise*` is final minus
draft). Bt and Bt0 tell both the Writer and the Reviewer the path (`pipeline.py`, `role != "researcher"`).
`list_files` sorts paths, so `docs/...` comes before `notes.md` and `00_notes.md` comes first. No semicolons, and no
mention of a modified vLLM. Fixes together add about 25 words, so recheck the 6-page limit.

| # | new text | issue | exact fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Methods, Runs: "A 14B \emph{first-listed run} adds the stored reply named \texttt{00\_notes.md}, told and untold." | "adds" suggests that the new arms are compared with the p17 told run's arms, which would make the comparisons cross-run. In fact, p18 reruns B and Bt in the same run, on the same 150 tasks and cap, and every first-listed comparison is within this run. The reader cannot tell this from the current wording. | "A 14B \emph{first-listed run}, on the same tasks and cap, interleaves tool-written notes, the told \texttt{notes.md}, and the stored reply named \texttt{00\_notes.md}, told and untold." |
| 2 | Statistics: "The told runs' comparisons are uncorrected, and the first-listed run's are also post hoc." | This is ambiguous. "also post hoc" can be read as "post hoc like the told runs", and it does not say clearly that the first-listed run's comparisons are uncorrected. | "The told and first-listed runs' comparisons are uncorrected, and the first-listed run's are post hoc." |
| 3 | Conclusion: "Naming the path helped at 32B. At 14B, the file also had to be listed first, and then only the Writer used it." | This overstates the result. The Reviewer opened `00_notes.md` in 15% of Bt0 tasks, so it is not true that "only the Writer used it". The sentence also leaves out the key result: the Reviewer was told the path and the file was listed first, yet the gain was revised away, and final accuracy rose only 1.1 (-2.4 to 4.9). So at 14B the rule "lead every later agent" was attempted, and the Reviewer did not follow. The only 14B evidence that leading the Reviewer would matter is the observational 23/127 split. Without this, the rule reads as supported at both sizes. | "Naming the path led both agents to the notes at 32B. At 14B, the Writer followed it only when the file was also listed first, and the Reviewer, which opened it in only \nRFirstReviewerOpenFirstTold{}\% of tasks, undid the Writer's gain." |
| 4 | Results, zero-copy timing: "Zero-copy thus cuts the prefill a deployment pays for but little of the time." | The trim dropped "in our runs". That turns a result from a few runs on one RTX PRO 6000 at 6 and 32 concurrent tasks into a general claim about deployments, which the paragraph's own title ("only with many concurrent tasks") contradicts. | "Zero-copy thus cuts the prefill a deployment pays for but, in our runs, little of the time." |
| 5 | Sensitivity, fan-out: "in two back-to-back runs. With three Researchers, ..." | Dropping "on one server" removes the condition that limits drift in the cross-run comparison of the third arm with one-Researcher B (-1.5, -5.8 to 3.0). The paper says that drift across GPUs is unmeasured, so the reader needs to know that both runs used the same server. | "in two back-to-back runs on one server." |
| 6 | Sensitivity, fan-out: "a difference in differences that removes common drift" | "common drift" can be read as "the usual drift". A difference in differences removes only drift that both channels share, so the trim drops a needed condition and overstates what the DiD removes. | "a difference in differences that removes drift common to both channels" |
| 7 | Results, zero-copy timing: "By vLLM's per-request counters in two runs, decoding takes ..." | The previous sentence names three runs, and the sentence after next has "In two runs with 32 tasks at a time", which are different runs. Without "of these", the decode-share runs can be read as yet another pair of runs. | "By vLLM's per-request counters in two of these runs, decoding takes ..." |
